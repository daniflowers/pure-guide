P2P (Main Levels)

In P2P, I recommend buying a house and completing the 'Gertrude's Cat' quest (and raising the kitten) as soon as possible. After that, you can complete 'Children of the Sun' so you can level up with the Gemstone Crab "mini-boss". Next, focus on leveling up your Ranged skill to 30 (I suggest aiming for 50 to prepare for 'Animal Magnetism,' which will also help you gain a decent amount of HP). Once that's done, you can prioritize the following skills needed for questing:

For Mining and Smithing, you can level up through questing. For Mining, complete "Doric's Quest" and "Plague City." For Smithing, just do "The Knight's Sword."

For Agility, I recommend completing "The Tourist Trap" and using both experience lamps on Agility to reach level 26. After that, train at the Brimhaven Agility Arena (using the camera plugin for convenience) or on an Agility course.

For Slayer, complete three quests to boost your level: "Porcine of Interest", "Recipe for Disaster - Mountain Dwarf" and the "Varrock museum-miniquest" After these quests, train Slayer as usual. You can use any Slayer master, but I recommend the one in Canifis.

For Magic, level it up however you prefer. Alternatively, you can wait until after "Horror from the Deep," but you’ll need to only use Ranged attacks during the fight. It’s not too difficult, just hide behind the pillars and attack each time the boss is green.

For Thieving, i recommend to start with the CoX (Chambers of Xeric) method from level 1 to 43. Once you hit 43, complete "The Feud" to reach level 45. Then, blackjack until level 50. After that, head to Varlamore and pickpocket Wealthy Citizens until level 53.

Monkey Madness 1 (No prayer)

I highly recommend having more than 35+ HP (the boss’s max hit) since the quest isn’t too difficult without Prayer, but I strongly suggest 50+ HP because you’ll need to tank some damage at times. I also recommend questing in World 330, where other players can sometimes tank for you. Always carry a teleport, stamina potions, antipoison, and good food in your inventory. Potatoes with cheese are usually cheap, but sharks work too.

The hardest part of this quest is navigating the monkey temple to make the bar. The gorillas can bodyblock you, so I suggest sticking behind the fire pits at the end of the path and waiting for them to move before heading down the trapdoor. Bring plenty of food because you can take a lot of hits at the bottom. Once you’re down the trapdoor, run to the safespot near the fire, heal up, and make the bar quickly.

If you have high enough HP, you can go back up to get the toy from the kid. Otherwise, teleport out to restock supplies—it’s your call. The other challenging part is the boss fight. The boss can two-shot you, so be careful and always keep your HP above 35. After entering the boss area, run straight to the bridge on the west side, cross it, and move slightly south so the boss can’t hit you. You can wait for NPCs to lower the boss’s HP if you want, but you must deal the final hit, or the boss will heal back to full health.

Mage Arena I & II (No Prayer)

I highly recommend having 80+ Magic and 65+ HP for Mage Arena I (MA1). It’s pretty easy to complete without Prayer, just use food during the boss fight and keep an eye out for PKers.

For Mage Arena II (MA2), you’ll need stamina potions since you’ll be running in the Wilderness while searching for the bosses. Use blighted food for healing (like blighted manta rays or karambwans) and blighted super restores. I strongly recommend bringing a super antipoison, as some boss locations have spiders, and their poison can easily kill you. After defeating each boss, bank the item or give it to the NPC. I suggest starting with Saradomin, Guthix, or Zamorak, but you can do them in any order you prefer.

Before each boss fight, remember to cast "Charge" to boost your Magic damage. For Saradomin, the best strategy is to hide behind a tree, attack each time your HP bar fade away, you can also try to bait his sword attacks, if you are fast enough you can hit him and hide behind the tree fast. Alternatively, you can attack the boss and run away, but you’ll need extra stamina potions for this. For Guthix, use a similar strategy, but watch out for spores that can heal the boss, prioritize destroying those first. For Zamorak, keep your distance, hit him, and run away. Bring plenty of stamina potions, as his special attack deals more damage the closer you are to him.

Desert Treasure I (No Prayer)

I highly recommend completing Mage Arena II (MA2) before starting this quest and having 80+ Magic. Bring the "Entangle" spell for the Smoke and Shadow Diamond bosses, it’s super useful for positioning.

The Smoke Diamond boss is really easy and doesn’t require brews of anything special besides not using an staff. Just use food and you’ll be fine.

The Shadow Diamond boss is trickier. Use "Entangle" to get into a good position. The best strategy is to hit a bat or rat to trap the boss, then focus on attacking only the boss. You can use a lower-level Entangle spell (like Snare) to trap the bat or rat. The boss has two phases, so take your time and don’t kill the bat or rat. You can also use the dog or skeletons to trap the boss, but they hit much harder, so be cautious.

For the Blood Diamond boss, trap the boss using the fences around the tomb. The boss will summon bats that deal 10 HP damage each time, but it’s manageable with food.

The Ice Diamond boss is the trickiest if you don’t have a tank account. Before entering the cave to reach this boss, resupply with Saradomin brews and super restores. Bring a fire spell lower than your current Magic level (e.g., Fire Blast if you’re 80+ Magic) and prepare for combat. A stamina potion is optional but helpful. After entering the cave, you’ll encounter a lot of wolves. I strongly recommend using an "NPC aggression timer" plugin to track their aggro range. Wait 10 minutes after the aggro timer expires to safely pass some wolves. For the next part, either use a stamina potion to run to the boss or tank some hits—always keep your HP high, as this is a multi-combat area. For the boss fight, brew up, restore your Magic, and keep your distance to avoid melee attacks. The boss will keep freezing you, but you can brew and use super restores to maintain your Magic level above the spell’s requirement. Kill the boss, and you’re done with this part.
